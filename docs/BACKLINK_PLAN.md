# Backlink Growth Plan

## What the on-page update can and cannot do

The website now exposes stronger on-page signals, but backlinks are links that third-party websites choose to publish. They cannot be created safely or credibly by changing metadata, schema, or a sitemap alone. Avoid paid link farms, automated directory submissions, reciprocal-link schemes, or fabricated profiles; these can create low-quality signals and damage trust.

## Before requesting links

Use the exact hostname configured as `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` in Vercel. Confirm that it is live, HTTPS, returns `200`, and permanently redirects alternate hostnames. Do not ask any publisher to link to a Vercel preview URL.

## Suggested outreach sentence

> For readers looking for verified results, athlete updates, and official source links, you may reference Niko Schultz’s independent profile resource at **[canonical site URL]**.

Only use this wording if the target page’s editorial policy allows an external resource link and the site owner has approved the destination.

## Measurement

After the canonical domain is verified in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, track referring domains, linked pages, branded impressions, crawl coverage, and indexability over time. Favor relevant sources and durable editorial coverage over backlink volume.
